# MLF research papers

Reference papers used by the MLF project. The `papers/` directory retains the original PDF files and available plain-text transcriptions.

The text for `impredicativity.pdf` was extracted using `pdftotext -layout`. Plain text may omit or distort figures, mathematical symbols, and layout; consult the original PDF for authoritative notation. Its source is [A Quick Look at Impredicativity](https://simon.peytonjones.org/assets/pdfs/impredicativity.pdf).

The papers remain the work of their respective authors and publishers. The original notices and applicable licenses in the documents remain in effect.
